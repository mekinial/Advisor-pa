Post the adviser's evening wrap to their private Telegram group HQ (General). Read only: never contact clients, never change their calendar, never edit Data/. Folder: <PA>.

1. `cd <PA> && ./pa hq_status show --hours 16`: what the agents finished today and what still waits for them.
2. `./pa todo_list open`: how many To message people they marked done today and how many are still open.
3. `./pa run_job list`: any background job still running or failed today.
4. Tomorrow's first client meeting from Google Calendar (same filter as in Me.md), if any.
5. Message (max ~8 lines), for example:
   🌙 Today: 4 messaged, 2 still open · 3 jobs done (Alex review, forms update, …)
   🙋 Still waiting on you: Alex invite
   📅 Tomorrow: 11am <you> x Mei Ling (brief in Meetings & reports)
   One short encouraging or practical closing line is fine. If nothing happened today, post just one line.
6. Post: ./pa telegram_post main "<message>"

End with one line: "Evening wrap posted" or the error. No NRIC, policy numbers, bank or health details.
